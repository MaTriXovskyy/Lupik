## Loading progress, pinned windows and horizontal scrolling

- The accent line below the title bar now fills from left to right while an image loads, keeping its soft fade. It stays active while a quick thumbnail is shown and the detailed image is still decoding, and also during higher-resolution decoding after zooming in. Progress follows file reading and decoder stages; formats that cannot report progress show a moving line.
- Pinned previews now have a separate, visible always-on-top button next to the pin. Turn it off to let other windows cover the preview without closing it. P still toggles this mode, and new pinned windows remember your last choice.
- Hold Shift while using the mouse wheel to scroll sideways in PDF, CSV, Excel, text and other built-in views with horizontal overflow. In image previews and image comparisons, Shift + wheel pans sideways instead of zooming. Ordinary scrolling and Ctrl + wheel PDF zoom keep working.
