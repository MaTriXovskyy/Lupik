## What's new

- Trim audio and video, and cut parts out of the middle: press E on a song or a video, set the start and end, drag over the timeline to select a part and press Delete. FFmpeg now comes with Lupik.
- Screenshot edits apply straight to the capture and go to the clipboard, without save dialogs: crop, arrows, frames, text. One Ctrl+Z brings back the capture before the change, with every mark still editable.
- Crop with C, like in Photoshop, with easier handles that never fall off the picture. Compare two selected files with V.
- A new screenshot bar at the top edge of the screen, with the 1–6 mode keys shown on the buttons.
